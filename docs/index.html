Every Club in the Year

How to read this section

Ten squares per club, one per DCP goal. On the year still running, filled means achieved, outlined means still reachable and flat means the window has closed for good. On a finished year nothing is reachable any more, so a square is filled or it is not.

Figures follow the dashboard's own count, in which the two officer-training rows earn a single goal, as do the two administrative rows. Select any row for the club's full record.

No Success Plan narrows the table to clubs the dashboard has not recorded a Club Success Plan for. It is a prerequisite for every recognition level, so a club without one cannot be Distinguished however many goals it meets.

A handful of archived years record fewer goal rows than the score the dashboard gave the club, almost always a blank Jun–Aug officer-training row. The score is the dashboard's own and stands, and those rows are marked because the squares cannot account for it.

Climbed is a club that finished under five goals and then beat that score; slipped is a club that was above five and fell back. Five is the Distinguished threshold, which is why it is the pivot.

A club can appear on both lists in different years; the record really does swing that way.

How to read this section

The trace shows the shape of each club's run — steady, spiking or sliding — against a dashed line at five goals. The last column is the year still running, so it is goals met so far rather than a year-end score, and it moves with each dashboard snapshot.

A club number survives a realignment and a district does not, so a club that transferred in brings its record with it. Those years show in a lighter figure, underlined: the score is the club's, but it was earned under the district that held it then, which the cell names on hover.
